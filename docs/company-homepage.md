# Customer page

The public site is `/`. Staff sign in at `/staff`. Operations and administrators edit what the page shows from **Customer site** in the workspace.

Rates, plants, freight, the hero, the about text, the contact lines, and the figure strip are stored records. A visitor’s quote and enquiry land on that same desk. The visitor can look up an order number. Only the office can move its stage.

The figures shipped with the brochure — trip counts, client counts, fleet partners, and the opening ex-plant rates — are a starting card. Change them in the workspace before treating them as the company’s record. Suppliers and drivers still request access at `/join`. Customer accounts can be requested on the public page and still need approval.
